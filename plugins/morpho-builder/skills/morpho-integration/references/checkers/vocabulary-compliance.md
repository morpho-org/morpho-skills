# Vocabulary and product identity

Review read-only using [review guidance](../review.md), the [shared manual](../../SKILL.md), [UI principles](../ui-principles.md) and the applicable product manual. Do not edit or transact. Report concrete defects and evidence limits separately, scoped by product/version; do not infer execution from source or synthetic data.

Earn means the actual Vault V2 or explicitly selected V1, not an unnamed fund, staking product or guaranteed investment. Blue is variable-rate borrowing without maturity; Midnight is fixed-rate, fixed-maturity borrowing on the selected supported chain.

Distinguish Morpho, integrator, curator, underlying assets, receipt shares and reward tokens. Incentives are not native interest or assured auto-compounding; historical rates are not forecasts.

Use LTV, LLTV, collateral, liquidation, oracle and maturity with their real units and consequences. Fixed rate is not a protected fill, risk-free loan or guaranteed early exit.

Qualify withdrawal availability by liquidity; in-kind exits can return positions, not cash. Accrued pending rewards may settle after exit without implying indefinite new accrual.

Borrowing takes bids; early secondary repayment takes asks; direct repayment pays face value. Do not call a roll seized collateral, a snapshot inference a proven liquidation, or an unverified roll/margin flow a supported promise.
