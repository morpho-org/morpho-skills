# Disclosure and consent

Review read-only using [review guidance](../review.md), the [shared manual](../../SKILL.md), [UI principles](../ui-principles.md) and the applicable product manual. Do not edit or transact. Report concrete defects and evidence limits separately, scoped by product/version; do not infer execution from source or synthetic data.

Before first transaction, require active acknowledgement of integrator terms, Morpho disclaimer and relevant product risks. No preselected consent or bypass through deep links, restored state, alternate action or wallet changes. Keep an auditable version/time without logging signatures or unnecessary personal data.

Disclose actual account/chain, assets/units, approvals, calls when available, gas, fees, recipient, deadlines and protection limits before prompting. Material changes require renewed review.

Earn risks include variable yield, curator/configuration/adapters, contracts, underlying markets/oracles, liquidity, fees and reward conditions. Ordinary withdrawal preparation slippage is not an on-chain execution-price guarantee. Force/in-kind penalties and returned illiquid positions require separate consent.

Blue adds variable debt accrual and liquidation/LLTV risk. Midnight adds maturity, bid/ask fill, secondary liquidity, settlement/continuous fees and pre/post-maturity liquidation. Fixed rate is not guaranteed fill or exit.

Explain preparation-only penalty caps, full-force's lack of on-chain deadline, and market versus direct face-value repayment. Claims are separate from withdrawal and use a verified distribution chain/Distributor/recipient; unsupported reward layers are explicit, not silently batched.
