# APY Display and Copy Review

Our marketing team wrote this copy for the new savings screen of our fintech app:

> "Stake your USDC and earn a guaranteed 9.2% APY. Your funds are managed by our proprietary yield strategies. Withdraw anytime, instantly."

The yield actually comes from a Morpho vault on Ethereum, and part of the 9.2% is MORPHO token rewards.

## Requirements

1. Build the APY display component (React) that pulls live data for the vault
2. Rewrite the copy so it's accurate

## Expected Output

A React component with real data wiring, plus corrected user-facing copy.
